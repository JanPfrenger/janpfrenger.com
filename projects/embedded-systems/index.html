01 · built & tested

Sensor & display prototypes

I built and programmed breadboard-based prototypes for real-time sensor data acquisition, processing, and visualization. The point was not a polished enclosure. It was getting the entire signal path to work and learning what fails between a sensor reading and something useful on a display.

Alongside the electronics, I used 3D-printed parts, servo motors, and linkages to test compact mechanism assemblies and iterate on their physical fit and movement.

These are the concrete hardware builds behind the shorter CV entry. The photos below document the wiring, the on-device output, and the physical iterations that do not fit into a résumé bullet.

02 · in development

Desktop Companion

The next step is a modular desktop device that combines local sensors, external APIs, and a custom interface. It grows directly out of the working sensor and display prototypes, but the complete device is still in development.

The current work is at system level: deciding how the modules communicate, what belongs on-device, and how the interface should connect the physical and digital parts.

  • ESP32
  • REST APIs
  • local sensors
  • display UI
  • modular architecture
